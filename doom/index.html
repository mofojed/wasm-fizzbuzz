DooM

A wasm32 port of 1997 linuxdoom-1.10 with minimal modifications to the sources, plus sound effects and OPL music. Find all the details in the README of the WebAssembly from Scratch Article.

Plays the shareware episode of DOOM by default. To play DOOM II, load your own IWAD: (it stays in your browser). The Ultimate DOOM, Final DOOM, and Freedoom work too. If you serve this page yourself, you can also put doom2.wad next to it and open ?wad=doom2.wad.

Sound starts once you click the DOOM canvas or press a key, since browsers block audio until then.

Use to start the game, arrow keys to move, to shoot, spacebar to open gates, and arrow keys to strafe (if your browser does not handle these keys otherwise). On mobile (and mobile only) touch the buttons above. Yeah, I'm not good a UX design.
